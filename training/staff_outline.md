# Staff Portal Training Outline

Per page: purpose, who uses, main actions.
Headings in English + Hindi.

## Staff Login / स्टाफ लॉगिन
- **Purpose**: Staff authentication with MFA
- **Who uses**: Staff members (ops, kyc, finance, sales, etc.)
- **Main actions**: Username + password → TOTP/2FA → staff dashboard
- **Notes**: 5 failed attempts → 15 min lockout. Super-admin accounts cannot login here.

## Staff Dashboard / स्टाफ डैशबोर्ड
- **Purpose**: Role-based landing page with relevant widgets
- **Who uses**: All staff roles
- **Main actions**: View assigned tasks, recent activity, quick links
- **Notes**: Navigation menu generated by `staffNavForRole()` — only role-appropriate pages shown

## Role-Based Access / भूमिका-आधारित पहुँच
- **Super-admin**: All pages (gateway_settings, reason_maps, audit_log, etc.)
- **CEO**: All pages except security-critical ones
- **Ops**: Merchants, KYC, transactions, support, disputes
- **KYC**: KYC review only — cannot edit partner keys or financial settings
- **Finance**: Settlements, payouts, reconciliation, financial reports
- **Regional Manager / Area Sales / Team Leader**: Merchant management, limited KYC
- **Field Staff**: View merchants, limited actions

## Blocked Actions (super-admin only) / अवरुद्ध क्रियाएँ
- Partner key edit (gateway_settings.php)
- Reason map management (admin_reason_map.php)
- Ledger state machine (admin_ledger_state.php)
- Platform wallet (admin_platform_wallet.php)
- Audit log (admin_audit_log.php)
- PII reveal (admin_pii_reveal.php)

## Staff Activity Logging / स्टाफ गतिविधि लॉग
- All staff actions are logged via `logStaffActivity()` — visible in admin_staff_activity.php
- KYC actions: verify, reject, hold, resubmit — all logged
- Cannot be edited or deleted (immutable audit trail)
